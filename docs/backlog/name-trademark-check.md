# Trademark check for the name Pantin

Phase: spikes and checks. ADR: none; spike 0004.

## Why

Spike 0004 found the name usable, but the trademark databases (INPI, EUIPO)
block robots, so classes 9 and 42 were not checked. It is the main open risk
before publishing under the name.

## What

By hand, by the user: search INPI and EUIPO (Nice classes 9 and 42) for
"Pantin" and the fallback "Homunculus", then note the result in spike 0004.
Nothing is published under the name before then (CLAUDE.md 4).
